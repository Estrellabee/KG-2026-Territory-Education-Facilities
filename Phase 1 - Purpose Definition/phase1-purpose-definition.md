# Phase 1 - Purpose Definition

## Project Name
Territory and Education Facilities

## Purpose
The purpose of this knowledge-graph project is to engineer a Knowledge Graph which models geographical entities and education-related facilities within a subset of the Trentino region, focusing on municipalities around Trento and Rovereto. Built upon a concrete set of user-oriented Competency Questions, the graph supports citizens in exploring the distribution of various educational institutions across local municipalities. Only local educational sites, including kindergartens, primary schools and secondary schools, are considered in the scope of this project.

## Persona
Anna is a parent who plans to move to southern Trentino. She needs to collect information about local educational services for her children before choosing which municipality to settle in.

## Scenario
Anna is comparing towns near Trento and Rovereto. She wants to find out what educational facilities are available in each municipality: which towns provide kindergartens or primary schools, and which towns lack secondary-school resources. She will run queries against this knowledge graph to support her family relocation decision-making.

## Competency Questions (CQs)
1. What types of educational facilities exist in municipalities around Trento and Rovereto?
2. Which educational facilities are located within a given municipality, for example Rovereto?
3. Which municipalities have available kindergarten facilities?
4. Which municipalities do not have secondary-school institutions?
5. What are the addresses and geographic coordinates of a selected educational facility?
